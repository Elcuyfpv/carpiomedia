Residential Real Estate Photography

Premium Residential Real Estate Photography Solutions

We specialize in capturing properties from every angle—combining crisp, high-resolution ground photography with cinematic aerial visuals to showcase your listings in the most compelling way.

Our ground-level shots highlight the design, detail, and atmosphere of a space, while our state-of-the-art drone technology adds scale, context, and perspective—revealing property size, layout, and surrounding views in a way traditional photos can’t.

About Carpio Media

Drone, FPV & Visual Production in NYC

Carpio Media creates visual work that makes a property, brand, or event feel impossible to ignore. Based in Forest Hills, Queens, we work with real estate professionals, brands, agencies, production teams, and local organizations that want more than a basic camera angle.

Every project begins with clear goals, thoughtful planning, and a creative approach shaped around the people who need to see the finished work.
