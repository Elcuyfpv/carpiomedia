MEET THE PILOT

Juan Carpio

Airline-captain experience behind every flight. Production judgment behind every shot.

Before founding Carpio Media, Juan spent approximately 17 years as a professional airline pilot and instructor captain, flying Airbus aircraft internationally and accumulating more than 9,000 flight hours.

He began flying FPV drones in 2018 and built Carpio Media to bring aviation-level planning, safety and reliability into commercial production. Juan personally leads the flying and visual execution for agencies, production companies, brands, real-estate teams, construction projects, events and public-sector clients.
