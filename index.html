MEET THE PILOT

Juan Carpio

Airline-captain experience behind every flight. Production judgment behind every shot.

Before building Carpio Media, Juan spent approximately 17 years as a professional airline pilot and instructor captain, flying Airbus aircraft internationally and accumulating more than 9,000 flight hours.

He began flying FPV drones in 2018 and founded Carpio Media to bring that aviation discipline into commercial production. Today, the company supports agencies, production houses, brands, real-estate teams, construction projects, events and public-sector clients across New York and the Tri-State Area.
